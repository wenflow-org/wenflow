# 我的英语错题本（高二 · 2026 春季学期）

> 每条格式：题目 → 我的答案（✗）→ 正确答案（✓）→ 当时老师讲的点

## 时态类

1. By the time we arrived, the movie ___ (start). → 我填 started ✗ → had started ✓ → 「过去的过去」要用过去完成时，关键是找 by the time + 过去时这个信号。
2. She ___ (live) here since 2019. → lived ✗ → has lived ✓ → since + 过去时点，主句用现在完成时。
3. I ___ (finish) my homework before my mom came back. → finished ✗ → had finished ✓ → before 连接的两个过去动作，先发生的用过去完成时。
4. Look! The children ___ (play) football on the playground. → played ✗ → are playing ✓ → Look!/Listen! 开头提示动作正在进行。

## 介词类

5. He has been ill ___ last Monday. → from ✗ → since ✓ → since + 时点，for + 时间段。
6. The book was written by a famous writer ___ the 1990s. → in the early 1990 → in the early 1990s ✓ → 年代前用 in，the 不能丢。
7. I'm looking forward to ___ (hear) from you. → hear ✗ → hearing ✓ → look forward to 里的 to 是介词，后接动名词。

## 从句类

8. ___ he said at the meeting surprised everyone. → What ✗（我填了 That）→ What ✓ → 主语从句缺宾语用 what，that 不成分。
9. This is the museum ___ we visited last year. → where ✗ → which/that ✓ → visit 是及物动词，从句缺宾语用关系代词，不是地点状语。
10. I'll wait ___ you come back. → until ✗（我填了 when）→ until ✓ → 句意是"等到……回来"。
11. The reason ___ he was late was that the bus broke down. → why ✗（我填了 that）→ why ✓ → the reason 后接 why 定语从句。

## 非谓语类

12. ___ (see) from the hill, the city looks beautiful. → Seen ✗（我填了 Seeing）→ Seen ✓ → 城市是被看，用过去分词表被动。
13. He hurried to the station, only ___ (find) the train had left. → finding ✗ → to find ✓ → only to do 表示出乎意料的结果。

## 词汇辨析类

14. The company ___ (提供) free lunch for employees. → provides（我拼成 provide for）✗ → provides ✓ → provide sth for sb / provide sb with sth。
15. His advice is ___ (value). → value ✗ → valuable / of value ✓ → 这里要形容词作表语。

## 完形填空高频错

16. （2026-03 月考完形）"The old man lived alone, but he never felt ___." → alone ✗ → lonely ✓ → alone 客观独处，lonely 主观孤独，语境是"从不感到孤独"。
17. （2026-04 月考完形）"She managed to ___ the difficulty." → pass ✗ → overcome/get over ✓ → 克服困难用 overcome，pass 是"通过考试/传递"。

## 作文扣分点

18. 作文里写了 "I very like English." → ✗ → I like English very much. ✓ → very 不直接修饰动词。
19. 作文里写了 "Although he is young, but he can do it." → ✗ → Although 与 but 不能连用 ✓ → 中文"虽然…但是"直译陷阱。
20. 作文里写了 "There have many people..." → ✗ → There are many people ✓ → there be 句型不能用 have。

## 复习备注
- 错题集中在：时态判断（4 条）、从句连接词（4 条）、固定搭配（5 条）。
- 完形和作文是重灾区，语法选择题正确率反而还行。
